
The 12 steps are the motivational element to achieve the following mechanical reality.

1. subdivide the addiction into its component parts
  - break apart each ritual/routine
  - indicate any chemical dependency (e.g., nicotine)
  - e.g., smoking cigarettes:
     a. nicotine fix
     b. routine of going outside
     c. routine of drawing out a cigarette (pull out pack, knock it, pull cigarette out and place in mouth, pull out lighter, light cigarette)
     d. reward of vasoconstriction in lungs
2. use [self-awareness] to define what you enjoy
    - the motivation is defined by how we derive pleasure
    - if you dig deep enough, it'll often have a broad-reaching [emotional] connection (e.g., striking to get the cigarette out reminds someone of their dad)
    - this is very emotionally draining, and often difficult to parse
3. find a replacement for the desirable mechanisms
    - this is easily done if you've sufficiently broken things apart
    - e.g., smoking cigarettes: gum for the routine, nicotine patches for the vasoconstriction
    - often, given our social needs, we need some level of human connection when we engage in our substance (which is why accountability partners are a thing)
4. give it all to God
    - God will grant the wisdom to see where you're missing something
5. if you relapse, you missed a component you desire and need to go back to find it
