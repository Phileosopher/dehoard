
God's "universe system" is composed of several components:

1. A series of general laws and rules that work autonomously (the "likely" things that happen, such as gravity, star movement, animal behavior, [human universals])
2. an override feature to govern whatever He wants (i.e., "miracles")

this system is in subjection to God's purposes, which is for the growth of the elect

- wicked beings, [the devil] included, are effectively tools to that end
- when evil people prosper, it is because He is using the system for a greater purpose ("salt and light" analogy, Elisha cleansing Jericho's waters)

another design feature of the system is that its override feature and His presence is fascinatingly hidden from us

- the only time we start seeing it is when we start playing with quantum mechanics, which is us poking at the implementation end of the switch (e.g., someone inside a piston making a spark plug engage)
